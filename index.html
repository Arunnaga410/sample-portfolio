❓ FAQ

Before We Start, You Might Be Wondering...

How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects take 2-4 weeks depending on complexity and scope. Simple websites take 1-2 weeks, while more complex projects with e-commerce or custom features can take 4-8 weeks.

What if I want to make changes after launch?

All packages include support and revision period. You can request changes during this time. After that, I offer maintenance packages to keep your website updated and running smoothly.

Do you design the website first or build it directly?

For larger projects, I create detailed designs in Figma that you can review and approve before I start building. For smaller projects, I may build using modern frameworks while maintaining design consistency.

Is my website SEO-friendly?

Yes! All websites are built with SEO best practices including proper heading structure, meta tags, mobile optimization, fast loading times, and semantic HTML.

Do you provide hosting and domain services?

I can recommend reliable hosting providers and help with setup. Domain registration can be handled independently or I can assist with the process.

What happens if I don't have content ready?

No problem! I can help you organize your content, create copy, or use placeholder content while you finalize your message. We work through it together.

Can you add new features after launch?

Absolutely! I can add new features, pages, integrations, or functionality based on your evolving needs. I offer ongoing support packages for this.

What makes you different from other web designers?

I focus on creating websites that serve a real purpose. Every design decision is tied to your goals. I combine beautiful design with clean code, responsive functionality, and genuine partnership throughout the process.
